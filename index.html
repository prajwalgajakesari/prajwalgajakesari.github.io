    Rally · monorepo

    all-things-rally

    A roadbook Studio. Import a GPX and it draws the tulips, distances and CAP headings for you. Includes an offline in-ride reader, plus grassroots stage timing from phones with no paid hardware. The symbols on this page's roadbook come from it.

    Motorsport · SaaS

    Lap

    A multi-tenant platform for FMSCI-sanctioned motorsport events in India: entries, officials, timing and results. Piloted with Moto Arena Adventure Park.

    iOS · Android · AI

    MotoGenie

    A riding companion for India. It does live GPS ride tracking, group rides with real-time location, and Live Activities on the lock screen. It sits on a dataset of 14,738 places and 3,246 routes enriched with seasons, permits and altitude, plus Genie, an AI co-rider built on Gemini.
